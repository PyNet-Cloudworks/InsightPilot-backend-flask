Welcome to InsightPilot

InsightPilot is a QA automation tool built for testing and analyzing logs, images, and videos — tailored for QA engineers, testers, and developers working with visual and diagnostic data daily.

🚀 What InsightPilot Offers

📄 Log File Analysis

Parse, highlight, and generate CSV summaries for quick debugging.

🖼️ Face & Defect Detection

Detect and annotate faces and defects in images. Includes YOLOv8 and similarity checks.

🎥 Video Insights

Analyze video files for frame-wise anomalies, face detection, and visual defects — all with downloadable reports.
